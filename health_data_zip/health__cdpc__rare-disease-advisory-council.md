# Rare Disease Advisory Council

*Health > CDPC > Rare Disease Advisory Council*

### Overview

The Indiana Rare Disease Advisory Council was established to conduct public meetings to survey the needs of patients in Indiana with rare diseases along with providing assistance to their caregivers and providers.
