# Division of Nutrition and Physical Activity

The Indiana Division of Nutrition and Physical Activity (DNPA) provides statewide leadership to support healthy eating and active living for Hoosiers of all ages. DNPA works to prevent and reduce obesity and other related chronic diseases by promoting evidencebased practices, strengthening partnerships, and creating environments where healthy choices are accessible and achievable for everyone.

Through collaboration with community organizations, schools, healthcare partners, and public health agencies, DNPA offers programs, training, and resources that advance best practices in nutrition and physical activity. These efforts are complemented by the work of the [Hoosier Health and Wellness Alliance](https://indianactsi.org/research/community/cinh/hhwa/), which brings together diverse stakeholders to advocate for policies, programs, and built environments that foster health, wellness, and vitality across Indiana.
