# Streptococcus (Group A) Invasive Disease

#### What is Group A Streptococcus ?

Group A *Streptococcus*(GAS) bacteria are contagious and can cause both minor and serious infections. GAS bacteria can cause inflammatory diseases. GAS bacteria can also be described as *Streptococcus* (Group A) or Streptococcal (Group A). Some individuals are at increased risk of GAS infection. To find out more please visit CDC’s webpage on [Group A Strep Infection](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/index.html).
