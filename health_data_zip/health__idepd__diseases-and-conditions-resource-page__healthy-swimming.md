# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: Healthy Swimming

## General Information

Swimming is a fun way to stay in shape and cool down after a hot day. However, recreational water illnesses and injuries can be serious, or even life-threatening. Keep you and your family safe by learning about illnesses, injuries, and ways to prevent them. Happy swimming!
