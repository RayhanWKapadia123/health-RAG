### Plan Review and Narrative

Ensuring transparency and accountability within the non-profit healthcare sector in Indiana is a cornerstone of the regulatory framework for CSHCR. Indiana Code (IC) 27-1-46.512 mandates specific requirements for hospitals operating within the State of Indiana. Certain non-profit hospitals are required to submit data to IDOH regarding Direct-to-Employer (DTE) Healthcare Arrangements, such that IDOH is able to conduct plan reviews to verify compliance with the statute.

Under IC 27-1-46.5-9, IDOH must verify that each obligated hospital offers a direct-to-employer healthcare arrangement that is at or below a benchmark of two hundred sixty percent (260%) of full Medicare by calculating the sum of inpatient facility prices and outpatient facility prices for each hospital and expressing these results as a percentage of full Medicare.

| --- | --- |

### Summary

Pursuant to IC 16-42-22.5-6, IDOH in consultation with the Indiana Board of Pharmacy, the Medical Licensing Board of Indiana, the Indiana State Board of Nursing, and the Office of the Attorney General, prepared the *Report on the Oversight of Drug Compounding* for the reporting period of July 1, 2026, to Aug. 31, 2026.

Pharmacy inspections were conducted and the following 27 violations were identified:

- six facilities found with expired medications on shelves,
- three facilities not properly executing the end-of-day dispensing verification process,
- nine violations for failure to follow federal controlled substance record-keeping requirements,
- two violations concerning pharmacy personnel qualifications,
- four facilities not meeting eligibility requirements for pharmacy permits, including lack of hot water at the time of inspection, and
- three violations for dispensing syringes without properly logging the transaction, whether electronically or by hardcopy.
