# Marriages

*Health > Vital Records > Marriages*

To obtain a certified copy of a marriage license or divorce decree, please contact the [Clerk of Court](https://www.in.gov/courts/local/) in the county where the marriage or divorce was issued.

If the county in which the marriage license was issued is unknown, a search may be conducted using:

1. Indiana Courts’ [Marriage License Public Lookup](https://publicaccess.courts.in.gov/mlpl/)
2. Indiana State Library’s [Indiana Marriages Database through 1850](https://digital.statelib.lib.in.us/legacy/search.aspx?index=3)
3. Indiana State Library’s [Indiana Marriages Database: 1958-2018](https://digital.statelib.lib.in.us/legacy/search.aspx?index=2)

For more information about applying for a marriage license, please visit the Indiana Court’s [Apply for a Marriage License](http://www.in.gov/judiciary/2605.htm) page. To purchase a copy of a Record of Marriage, complete and submit [State Form 54764](https://www.in.gov/dA/12b78a91f5/Marriage-Application-10.2018.docx?language_id=1).

For U.S. Citizens who were married, or intend to marry, abroad, please visit the U.S. Department of State’s [Marriage Abroad](http://travel.state.gov/content/passports/en/abroad/events-and-records/marriage.html) page for more information.
