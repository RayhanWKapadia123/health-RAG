Hepatitis C Serology

1. Acceptable specimens:

- Serum, minimum 3ml
- Whole blood collected in a serum separator (SST) tube (e.g. tiger-top, gold top) then centrifuged to separate serum

1. Specimens may be stored at 15-30°C for ≤ 7 days, 2-8°C for ≤ 7 days or -20°C or colder for longer for Anti-HCV.

1. Specimens must arrive at lab within 5 days of collection.

1. If frozen, specimens must be shipped on dry ice and arrive at lab still frozen. Avoid more than 3 freeze/thaw cycles.

1. Whole blood must never be frozen, or it will hemolyze. Hemolyzed, lipemic, or heparinized specimens cannot be tested per assay manufacturer requirements.

1. For HCV RNA testing: Specimens may be stored at 2-25°C for ≤ 24 hours, 2-8°C for ≤ 5 days, or from -20°C to -80°C for up to 10 weeks.

Laboratory reports will be sent out via LimsNet and will be available within three to five business days.
