# 410 IAC 6-21 Public and Semi-Public Pools

*Health > EPH > Laws, Rules and Forms > 410 IAC 6-21 Public and Semi-Public Pools*

## Swimming Pool Rule 410 IAC 6-2.1

Rule 410 IAC 6.2.1 has been revised to include the regulation of Tourist Homes and updated. The revised rule went into effect on August 26, 2010. Anyone involved with swimming pools should study the revised rule.

The revised rule is available [HERE](https://www.in.gov/dA/0a93e615cb/410_iac_6_2_1.pdf?language_id=1).

 <h3>Rule 2.1. Public and Semi-Public Pools</h3> <p>410 IAC 6-2.1-1 Applicability<br />Sec. 1. The definitions in this rule apply throughout this rule.</p> <p>410 IAC 6-2.1-2 "Air gap" defined<br />Sec. 2. "Air gap":<br />(1) means the unobstructed vertical distance through atmosphere between the water supply inlet and the flood level rim of the receiving unit; and<br />(2) is at least two (2) times the diameter of the water supply outlet or pipe or six (6) inches, whichever is the smaller distance.</p> <p>410 IAC 6-2.1-3 &ldquo;Bather load&rdquo; defined<br />Sec. 3. &ldquo;Bather load&rdquo; means the total number of bathers within the pool enclosure.</p> <p>410 IAC 6-2.1-4 &ldquo;Breakpoint chlorination&rdquo; defined<br />Sec. 4. &ldquo;Breakpoint chlorination&rdquo; means the point in a rising chlorine residual at which the concentration of available chlorine becomes great enough to completely oxidize all organic matter and ammonia compounds (combined chlorine) in a pool.</p>
