### Guide for Sanitary Operations of Tattoo And Body Piercing Facilities in Indiana

The safety of our fellow Hoosiers is very important to us.

If you feel uncomfortable during tattooing or body piercing procedures or suspect improper practices—please call your local county health department to share your concern(s).

If you think you have been the victim of a crime, please report this immediately to your local police.
