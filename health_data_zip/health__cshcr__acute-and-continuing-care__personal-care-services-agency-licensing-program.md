# Personal Care Services Agency (PSA) Licensing Program

*Health > CSHCR > Acute and Continuing Care > Personal Care Services Agency (PSA) Licensing Program*

## About the Personal Care Services Agency (PSA) Licensing Program

The Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) licenses agencies that provide attendant care services; homemaker services that assist with or perform household tasks, including housekeeping, shopping, laundry, meal planning and preparation, and cleaning; and companion services that provide fellowship, care, and protection for a client, including transportation, letter writing, mail reading, and escort services.

These services are provided to a client at the client's residence by the staff of a licensed personal care services agency (PSA).

The [IDOH Gateway](https://gateway.isdh.in.gov/Gateway/SignIn.aspx) is used to complete initial registrations, renewals, upload required documents, and complete payments for PSAs. The IDOH Gateway is accessed through [Access Indiana](https://access.in.gov/signin). Once access is gained to Access Indiana and the IDOH Gateway, users may request access to the *Personal Service Agency License Registration* application.

### Register for Gateway

1. [Click here](https://gateway.isdh.in.gov/Gateway/Signin.aspx) to access the IDOH Gateway.

1. If you do not have an account, on the right-hand side, click *Step 1 - New User Register with Access Indiana*("Register to Access Indiana").

- When prompted, select *Personal Service Agency* from the drop-down menu for applications.
2. If you have an account but need application access, on the right-hand side, click *Step 2 - Request for Gateway or New Application Access* ("Request Access").

- When prompted, select *Personal Service Agency* from the drop-down menu for applications.
3. If you have an account and have application access, on the right-hand side, click *Step 3 - Registered Users with Gateway and Access Indiana*("Sign in with Access Indiana").

Once registration is completed successfully, users will receive a confirmation message.

Once an administrator approves the registration, users will receive an email notification of approval. The approval email will include a registration identification (ID) number that users will use to sign into the IDOH Gateway.

1. [Click here](https://gateway.isdh.in.gov/Gateway/Signin.aspx) to log into the IDOH Gateway.

3. At the bottom left-hand corner of the page, click the *Register New Agency* button.

- Enter basic details of the agency; all fields are mandatory, including *Agency Hours*.

- The *Details* page is displayed, which includes payment amount and effective date of the license. Review and complete all details for the agency. Click *Next*.

5. Complete *Manager* details, then click *Next*.

- If the agency has multiple locations, click *Add Location* button to add additional locations.
7. Upload required documents in *Documents* section, then click *Next*.
8. Complete the *Attestation* section, then click the *Submit* button.
9. Enter transaction details. Click *Proceed*.
10. Enter payment details. Click *Submit Payment*.

- Once payment is processed, click *Continue* and a payment confirmation is displayed. An email confirmation is also sent to the email address on-file for the agency.

| ## License Renewal | [Click for Gateway Registration and Renewal Workflow](https://www.in.gov/dA/0605aded93/PSA Gateway - Renewal and Registration Workflow.pdf?language_id=1) |
| --- | --- |

The option for renewal becomes available at the 60-day mark for the current expiration of each agency. For example, if a license expires on Dec. 15, 2025, the agency will be able to renew their registration no earlier than Oct. 16, 2025.

1. [Click here](https://gateway.isdh.in.gov/Gateway/Signin.aspx) to log into the IDOH Gateway.

- On the right-hand side, click *Step 3 - Registered Users with Gateway and Access Indiana*("Sign in with Access Indiana").
2. Access the licensure application by clicking on the *Personal Service Agency License Registration* button.
3. Search for agency by its employer ID number or by its name.
4. When agency is located, click on *View Details*.
5. At the bottom left-hand corner of the page, click the *Renew* button.

- Review basic details of the agency; all fields are mandatory, including *Agency Hours*.
- When completed, click the *Next* button.
- The *Details* page is displayed, which includes payment amount and effective date of the license. Review and complete all details for the agency. Click *Next*.
6. Complete *Ownership* details, then click *Next*.
7. Complete *Manager* details, then click *Next*.
8. Complete the *Location* details, then click *Next*.

- If the agency has multiple locations, click *Add Location* button to add additional locations.
9. Upload required documents in *Documents* section, then click *Next*.
10. Complete the *Attestation* section, then click the *Submit* button.
11. Enter transaction details. Click *Proceed*.
12. Enter payment details. Click *Submit Payment*.

- Once payment is processed, click *Continue* and a payment confirmation is displayed. An email confirmation is also sent to the email address on-file for the agency.

Anyone may submit a complaint to IDOH about care provided at any licensed or certified Indiana healthcare facility or entity. IDOH is available to receive complaints against nursing homes, hospitals, home health agencies, hospice agencies, personal service agencies, and intermediate care facilities for persons with intellectual disabilities (ICF-IID; also called group homes). IDOH investigates all eligible complaints.

This report link is to a page that provides information about the complaint process and how to report a complaint. A *complaint* report form is provided for individuals to assist in the reporting of care issues.
