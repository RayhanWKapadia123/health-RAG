# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: Indiana Hepatitis A Outbreak

# Hepatitis A Outbreak

#### Situation Overview

Indiana was one of several states that experienced a [hepatitis A outbreak linked to person-to-person contact in the United States since 2016](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-a/outbreaks/person-to-person/index.html). Indiana’s hepatitis A outbreak spanned from December 1, 2017 to December 31, 2023. Hepatitis A usually spreads person-to-person when someone accidentally consumes stool (poop) of someone with hepatitis A or by consuming food or water contaminated with the virus. During this outbreak, hepatitis A was spread through person-to person transmission, especially among people who used drugs and people experiencing homelessness. More than half of the people who got sick were hospitalized.
