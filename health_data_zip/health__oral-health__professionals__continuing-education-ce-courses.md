# Continuing Education (CE) Courses

*Health > Oral Health > Resources for Professionals > Continuing Education (CE) Courses*

In order to participate in the continuing education program, you will need to create an [IN-TRAIN account](https://www.train.org/main/welcome) if you do not already have one.

Currently, there are no Continuing Education Courses offered by the IDOH Oral Health Program; however, there are 4 [Non-CE Courses](https://www.in.gov/health/oral-health/professionals/non-ce-courses/) provided by the Oral Health Program.

### Continuing Education Courses on IN-TRAIN

Health Equity: the pursuit of an equal opportunity for all people to achieve their best possible health.

- [Introduction to Health Equity & Racial Justice](https://www.train.org/indiana/course/1100784/) - Course ID 1100784
- [Achieving Health Equity](https://www.train.org/indiana/course/1100159/) - Course ID 1100159
- [Addressing Health Equity: A Public Health Essential](https://www.train.org/indiana/course/1041931/) - Course ID 1041931

Disparities in Oral Health by community: the inequalities that occur in the provision of healthcare and access to healthcare across different racial, ethnic and socioeconomic groups.

- [Measuring Health Disparities](https://www.train.org/indiana/course/1087494/) - Course ID 1087494
- [Rural Health: Exploring The Growing Disparities](https://www.train.org/indiana/course/1078208/) - Course ID 1078208
- [Serving Diverse Communities: Finding Data on Health Disparities](https://www.train.org/indiana/course/1079670/) - Course 1079670
