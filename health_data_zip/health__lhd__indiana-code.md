# Indiana Code

*Health > LHD > Indiana Code*

The following are Indiana Codes that govern Local Health Departments (IC 16-20).

**[IC 16-20-1](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-1) Powers and Duties of Local Health Departments**
Defines the powers and duties of Local Health Departments which apply to all local health officers and local health boards.

**[IC 16-20-2](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-2) Local Boards of Health**
Defines the composition of a Board of Health, including membership selection, appointment, term of office and removal.

**[IC 16-20-3](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-3) Multiple County Health Departments**
Defines the conditions should county executives of at least two (2) adjacent counties decide to establish and maintain a multiple county health department.

**[IC 16-20-4](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-4) City Health Departments in Second Class Cities**
Defines the formation, establishment, jurisdiction and authority of a city health department.

**[IC 16-20-5](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-5) Area Boards of Health**
Defines the authority of area boards of health including jurisdiction, membership, term of office, meetings and officers.

**[IC 16-20-5.5](https://iga.in.gov/laws/2025/ic/titles/16#16-20-5.5) Appeal of Local Health Enforcement Actions**
Defines the process for appealing to the legislative body an enforcement action issued or taken by a local board of health or local health officer under [IC 16-20-1](https://iga.in.gov/laws/2025/ic/titles/16#16-20-1)

**[IC 16-20-6](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-6)Acceptance of Gifts for County or City Health Department Buildings**
Defines the authority to accept gifts to erect and equip buildings, the form of the gifts and the construction and equipment of buildings.

**[IC 16-20-7](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-7) Assistance by Cities and Counties to Public Health Nursing Associations**
Defines the authority of how a city or county may appropriate money out of the general fund of the city or county to assist incorporated public health nursing associations.

**[IC 16-20-8](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-8) Food Service Inspections**
Defines the checklist and narrative report regarding food service establishment inspections.

**[IC 16-20-9](http://iga.in.gov/legislative/laws/2025/ic/titles/016/#16-20-9) Penalties**
Defines that a person who recklessly violates or fails to comply with this article commits a Class B misdemeanor.

**[IC 16-46-16.7](https://iga.in.gov/laws/2025/ic/titles/16#16-46-16.7) Core Public Health Services Contracts and Grants**
Applies to a local health department that is approved to receive additional funding as set forth in [IC 16-46-10-2.2](https://iga.in.gov/laws/2025/ic/titles/16#16-46-10-2.2).
