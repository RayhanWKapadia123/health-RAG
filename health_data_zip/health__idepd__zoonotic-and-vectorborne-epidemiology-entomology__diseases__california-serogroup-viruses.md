# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: California Serogroup Viruses

# California Serogroup Viruses

California (CAL) serogroup viruses are arthropod-borne viruses (arboviruses) that are primarily transmitted by mosquitoes. The most commonly reported CAL serogroup virus in Indiana is [La Crosse virus (LACV)](https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/zoonotic-and-vectorborne-epidemiology-entomology/zoonotic-diseases/la-crosse-virus). Other CAL serogroup viruses found in the United States include:

- California encephalitis virus
- [Jamestown Canyon virus](https://www.cdc.gov/jamestown-canyon/about/index.html)
- Snowshoe hare virus
- Trivitattus virus
