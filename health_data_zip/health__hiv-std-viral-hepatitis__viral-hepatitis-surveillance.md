# Viral Hepatitis Surveillance

*Health > HIV STD Viral Hepatitis > Viral Hepatitis Surveillance*

### Viral Hepatitis LHD Assistance Program

The Viral Hepatitis Surveillance student investigator program focuses on disease surveillance, prevention, and services for people 18-39 living with hepatitis B and C in Indiana through assisting in case investigations. Local health departments can sign up for this assistance program through filling out the agreement form below and sending it back to IDOH.

- [2026-2027 VH SI Assistance Agreement Form](https://www.in.gov/dA/82fb0073b4/2026-2027-MOU.pdf?language_id=1)
- [2026-2027 VH SI Investigation Workflow](https://www.in.gov/dA/1b5680c36d/2025-2026-VH-SI-Investigation-Workflow.pdf?language_id=1)
