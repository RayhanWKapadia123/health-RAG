# Healthcare Quality Resource Center

*Health > CSHCR > Healthcare Quality Resource Center*

The Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) Health Care Quality Resource Center is intended to provide resources and information to assist health care facilities in providing quality health care. The information may also be educational to patients and families who wish to learn more about care issues.

### Healthcare Quality Improvement Project Center

The IDOH develops healthcare quality improvement projects as part of its Long Term Care Civil Money Penalty (CMP) Program. The following are web pages for quality improvement projects developed through the CMP Program:

- [Civil Money Penalty Project Center](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/healthcare-quality-resource-center/civil-money-penalty-project-center)
