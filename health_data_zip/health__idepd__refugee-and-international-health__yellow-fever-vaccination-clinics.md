# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: Yellow Fever Vaccination Clinics

***Update:** Effective April 5, 2021, YF-VAX® (Yellow Fever Vaccine) is available in the U.S. for authorized YF-VAX providers. Yellow Fever Vaccine must be administered at least 10 days before your travel. To meet your travel-related vaccination needs, please search the Indiana Authorized YF-VAX Vaccine providers list below. You will need to contact the clinic to schedule an appointment. Fees may vary by clinic.

**Inclusion of travel clinics listed below should not be considered as an endorsement of a specific provider from the Indiana Department of Health.**
