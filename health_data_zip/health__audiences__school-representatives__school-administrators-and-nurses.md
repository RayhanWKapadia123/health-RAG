##### Wellness Policy Evaluations[[image: wellsat]](https://www.wellsat.org/upload/docs/WellSAT%20Hardcopy.pdf)

The [Division of Nutrition and Physical Activity](https://www.in.gov/health/dnpa/home) (DNPA) offers year-round reviews of corporation wellness policies. School liaisons can submit their corporation’s wellness policy to DNPA for review. DNPA utilizes the [WellSAT 3.0 Quantitative Assessment Tool](https://www.wellsat.org/upload/docs/WellSAT%20Hardcopy.pdf) to score and improve the local School Wellness Policy and then will schedule a consultation meeting to review results and match school liaisons with support and resources!
