# Each woman is unique, but when it comes to motherhood, we all have questions.

## Moms Helpline helps Indiana women get the answers and resources they need.

Whether you’re looking for an OBGYN, diapers, or a place to ask parenting questions, our Communication Specialists will be there to assist you—because every woman should feel supported during motherhood. We connect you with resources within your community and from state agencies to help you and your child live happy and healthy lives.

## More About Moms Helpline

- What kind of questions can the Moms Helpline answer?

Moms Helpline can answer questions about prenatal care, pregnancy, parenting, and infant/toddler development. We can also speak with you about where to get free baby supplies and other resources. Additionally, you can reach out to us to learn more about finding a doctor or health insurance. Call us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667) to get your questions answered.
- How do I know if I am eligible to use Moms Helpline?

Anyone in Indiana who is 16 or older can contact Moms Helpline. Call us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667).
- Is there really no cost?

Moms Helpline is completely free. You can reach us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667). We are open Monday through Friday between 8:30 am-7 pm.
- I have a private question. Will Moms Helpline keep our conversation confidential?

Moms Helpline calls are confidential and your conversation will be kept private. Although, if you are in need of a specific resource (like health insurance), you may need to share some relevant personal information so that we can properly assist you. Call us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667) to get your questions answered. We’re open Monday through Friday from 8:30 am to 7 pm.
- Can you help me find free or discounted diapers, formula, baby clothes, food, etc?

Yes, Moms Helpline can connect you with organizations offering free or discounted baby supplies and food.
- I need car seat assistance. Can Moms Helpline help with that?

Yes, Moms Helpline can refer you to an organization that provides free or discounted car seats, as well as car seat safety checkpoints. Call us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667). We’re open Monday-Friday between the hours of 8:30 am and 7 pm.
- Can you help me find an OB/GYN, pediatrician, or family medicine provider?

Yes, Moms Helpline can help you find the right provider for you and your family. You can reach us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667) Monday through Friday from 8:30 am to 7 pm.
- How quickly can I get resources?

Our Communication Specialists can connect you immediately to resources near your area. Please note, resource availability varies based on the agency and some require medical provider referral or have scheduling limitations. But Moms Helpline can get you started right away. Give us a call at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667).
- Who runs the Moms Helpline?

The Indiana Department of Health runs Moms Helpline. Call us at 1-844-624-6667 (18446246667). Our Communication Specialists will be happy to assist you Monday through Friday between 8:30 am and 7 pm.
