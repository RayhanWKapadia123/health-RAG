Recommended Testing for All Private Well Water

**Well water should be tested annually for Bacteria and Nitrate.**

At least every 5 years well water should also be tested for **Fluoride, Arsenic, Lead and Copper** or whenever any major maintenance is done.

If any of the above tests come back elevated, we recommend additional testing.
