RRTs (Rapid Response Teams) are multi-agency, multi-disciplinary teams that operate using Incident Command System (ICS)/National Incident Management System (NIMS) principles and a Unified Command structure to respond to human and animal food emergencies. The Indiana RRT helps support the mission of the Indiana Department of Health Food Protection Division to promote and support a healthier and safer Indiana by striving for a safe food supply.
