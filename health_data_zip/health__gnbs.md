# Public Health Genetics

The Public Health Genetics (PHG) is a program within the Maternal and Child Health division at the Indiana Department of Health that houses the **[Newborn Screening (NBS) Program](https://www.in.gov/health/gnbs/gnbs-programs/newborn-screening-program)**, the **[Early Hearing Detection and Intervention (EHDI) Program](https://www.in.gov/health/mch/families/ehdi-early-hearing-detection-and-intervention/)**, the [**Indiana Birth Defects and Problems Registry** (**IBDPR)**](https://www.in.gov/health/gnbs/gnbs-programs/birth-defect-and-nbs-condition-info/ibdpr-indiana-birth-defects-and-problems-registry) and the **[Autism and Developmental Disabilities Monitoring (ADDM) Network](https://www.in.gov/health/gnbs/gnbs-programs/autism-and-developmental-disabilities-monitoring-addm-network)**. The PHG Program is responsible for ensuring timely and quality newborn screening occurs for each and every Hoosier baby.
