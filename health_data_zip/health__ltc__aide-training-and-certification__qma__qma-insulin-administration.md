# QMA Insulin Administration

*Health > LTC > Aide Training, Certification, and Registry > Qualified Medication Aide (QMA) Training and Certification > QMA Insulin Administration*

### Important Update

Effective January 2025, IDOH has made a few additional updates to the QMA-Insulin Administration Module. The updated student and instructor QMA-Insulin Education Modules may be accessed at the links below.

### Announcements

Effective 12/31/2019 the Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) – Division of Long-Term Care released the Qualified Medication Aide (QMA) – Insulin Administration Education Module. This education module is approved to train current and future QMAs in the administration of insulin to residents in Indiana licensed healthcare facilities. The Insulin Administration Education Module must be conducted through an Indiana approved Qualified Medication Aide Training Program by an approved Program Director. The training must follow the approved curriculum.

The Insulin Administration Education Module is an OPTIONAL training for current and future QMAs.

The training includes:

- 4-8 hours of classroom training following the state approved curriculum
- 2-4 hours of practical training with 1:1 supervision of licensed registered nurse
- Successful completion of skills competency checklist with 100% accuracy administered by the QMA training program approved Program Director
- Successful completion of written competence examination administered by the state approved testing entity.
- The approved QMA training program is responsible for maintaining a student file for each individual.

### Qualified Insulin Administration Testing

Testing is administered through Ivy Tech Community College. Ivy Tech Community College has a CNA/QMA Central Testing Office located in Indianapolis that processes all applications and schedules testing throughout the state. This office is staffed by Central Coordinators.

The testing application is completed online. Please go to [Ivy Tech Community College’s website](https://www.ivytech.edu/student-services/support-services/testing-services/cna-qma-home-health-aide-testing-information/) for more information.

As of January 1, 2025, the fee for the Insulin Administration Certification written (computer-based) test is $75.00. You may contact Ivy Tech Central Testing Office (cna_qma_testing@ivytech.edu).
