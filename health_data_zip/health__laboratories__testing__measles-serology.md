Measles Serology

1. Acceptable specimens:

- serum, minimum 600µL; 1 mL preferred.
- whole blood collected in a serum separator (SST) tube (e.g. tiger-top, gold top) then centrifuged to separate serum.
2. Specimens must be stored refrigerated (2 - 8°C) or frozen (-20°C or colder).
3. Refrigerated specimens must be shipped on frozen ice packs and arrive at the lab within 48 hours of collection.
4. Frozen specimens must be shipped on dry ice and arrive at lab still frozen. Avoid unnecessary freeze/thaw cycles.
5. Whole blood must never be frozen, or it will hemolyze. Hemolyzed, lipemic, or heparinized specimens cannot be tested per assay manufacturer requirements.

1. Serum/blood collection materials, including SST, tiger-top, or gold-top tubes
2. Ice packs
3. Shipping boxes or containers with appropriate shipping labels

1. UN3373 Biological Substances, Category B shipping container
2. 9A shipping container provided by IDOH
4. Access to LimsNet online account for entry submission

1. Wrap the labeled specimen tube with absorbent material and place in the inner shipping canister.
2. Place LimsNet submission cover page in outer container or in plastic bag to prevent contamination from specimen.
3. Place the inner shipping canister and submission cover page into the outer shipping canister.
4. Specimens should be shipped to arrive at IDOH Labs Monday through Friday. It is not recommended to ship specimens which will be in transit over the weekend or holiday.
5. Specimen temperature requirements must be maintained during shipping. Insulated shipping boxes with frozen ice packs will be necessary for refrigerated specimens or dry ice to maintain frozen specimens.
