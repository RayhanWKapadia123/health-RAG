# Vector-Borne Diseases

*Health > IDEPD > Vector-Borne and Zoonotic Disease > Vector-Borne Diseases*

Vector-borne diseases are human illnesses caused by bacteria, parasites, and viruses that are transmitted by infected arthropods such as mosquitoes, ticks, and other pests. There are a variety of vector species that transmit diseases in Indiana.
