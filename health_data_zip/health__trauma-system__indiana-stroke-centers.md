### Indiana Certified Stroke Centers

*Select a county to view locations*

Starting July 2018, per IC 16-31-2-9.5, the Indiana Department of Health is responsible for compiling and maintaining a list of certified stroke centers and hospitals, as well as stroke network participating hospitals in Indiana. In order for stroke centers to appear on this list, they must provide:

- What level of stroke certification (Comprehensive Stroke Center, Thrombectomy-Capable Stroke Center, Primary Stroke Center, or [Acute] Stroke-Ready Hospital),
- Name of the certifying entity, and
- Provide proof of certification by including a copy of the stroke center certification and the date the certification is set to expire.

If there is a change in stroke center status, (such as suspended, revoked, or lowered) the law requires hospitals to inform IDOH of such action within 48 hours of such action.

Network participating hospitals must provide a copy of their written transfer agreements in order to be included on the list. Hospitals that are certified as Stroke-Ready have transfer agreements with either a Comprehensive or Primary Stroke Center.

There are several organizations that provide stroke certification. The naming mechanisms for the levels of stroke certification are slightly different. For more information about the levels of stroke certification, visit their websites:

- [DNV GL Healthcare:](https://www.dnv.us/services/stroke-care-certification-programs-219582)
- Comprehensive Stroke Center Certification
- Primary Plus Stroke Center Certification
- Primary Stroke Center Certification
- Acute Stroke Ready Certification
- [The Joint Commission](https://www.jointcommission.org/certification/dsc_neuro2.aspx):
- Comprehensive Stroke Center Certification
- Thrombectomy-Capable Stroke Center
- Primary Stroke Center Certification
- Acute Stroke Ready Hospital Certification

- [Accreditation Commission for Health Care](https://www.achc.org/):
- Comprehensive Stroke
- Thrombectomy Stroke
- Primary Stroke
- Stroke Ready

To be added to the list of stroke centers, please send certification information to Director Brian Busching, BBusching@health.in.gov.
