# Dental Health Curriculum for K-2

*Health > Oral Health > Dental Health Curriculum for K-2*

The primary goal of this initiative is to incorporate oral health education into the school curriculum, empowering students with the knowledge to maintain good oral hygiene. Educate students on the importance of good oral hygiene and preventive care. Build healthy habits early, reducing the risk of future dental issues. Engage students in understanding the link between oral health and overall well-being. Incorporating an oral health curriculum can improve the early identification of dental issues and help foster lifelong healthy habits in students. Let's champion oral health as an integral part of holistic care, ensuring every child receives the attention their smiles deserve.
