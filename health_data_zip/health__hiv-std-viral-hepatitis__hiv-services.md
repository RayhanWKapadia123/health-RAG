# HIV Services

*Health > HIV STD Viral Hepatitis > HIV Services*

The HIV Services Program (HSP) provides eligible people living with HIV (PLWH) in Indiana access to antiretroviral medications and other important core medical and support services. HSP was expanded in 2018. Traditionally designed to give an individual full access to comprehensive health insurance at no cost, the program now offers a range of services to PLWH enrolled by non-medical case management organizations across Indiana.

### Other Programs Offered

<p style="-ms-text-justify:newspaper; -ms-text-kashida-space:50%; margin-bottom:0pt; text-align:justify"

Each aspect of HSP below contains important information for HIV providers (sub-recipients) funded through the [Ryan White HIV/AIDS Program (RWHAP)](https://hab.hrsa.gov/about-ryan-white-hivaids-program/about-ryan-white-hivaids-program) Part B and for PLWH in Indiana.

- AIDS Drug Assistance Program (ADAP)

- This program is a placeholder while a client is connected to traditional insurance. The program covers HIV-specific medications and other limited mediations.
- Early Intervention Plan (EIP)

- The program covers limited medical services such as doctor visits, laboratory services, specified vaccinations, and influenza shots. EIP provides funding for limited health care services during the waiting period before other insurance coverage begins.
- Health Insurance Assistance Plan (HIAP)

- This program pays the premium, deductible, co-pay, and co-insurance costs to eligible individuals routed through participating Indiana Qualified Health Plans.
- Medicare Part D Assistance Plan (MDAP)

- This program provides assistance towards the co-pay, co-insurance and deductible costs of a Medicare Part D prescription drug plan for qualifying individuals.
- Healthy Indiana Plan (HIP) Assistance

- Indiana offers a Medicaid program called the Healthy Indiana Plan. There are two program options that can help cover the monthly Power Account Payment in the Health Indiana Plus program as well as co-pays and deductibles in the Health Indiana Basic program.
- Services Only

- This program is for clients that have their own insurance coverage but are eligible for other funded supportive services like food pantry or housing assistance services.
