# Agricultural Labor Camps Roster

*Health > EPH > Agricultural Labor Camps > Agricultural Labor Camps Roster*

The online searchable database below provides the information for agricultural labor camps inspected by the Indiana Department of Health’s Environmental Public Health Division. Agricultural labor camps are subject to inspection that provide living quarters for five or more adult seasonal or temporary workers engaged in agricultural activities or food processing.
