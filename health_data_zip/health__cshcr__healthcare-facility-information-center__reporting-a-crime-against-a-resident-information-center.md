# Reporting a Crime Against a Resident Information Center

*Health > CSHCR > Healthcare Facility Information Center > reporting-a-crime-against-a-resident-information-center*

If long-term care facility staff or personnel suspect a crime has been committed against a resident of, or someone receiving care from, a long-term care facility, the staff/ personnel are required to report any reasonable suspicion of a crime to the Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) and at least one local law enforcement entity.

 For consumers needing to submit a complaint on a licensed health care facility or entity, please visit [Reporting a Complaint about a Health Care Facility](https://www.in.gov/?a=398497).

 To report an incident in a long-term care facility, please visit [Incident Reporting by Long Term Care Facilities](https://www.in.gov/health/ltc/incident).

### How to Report

The [Reporting a crime against a resident form](https://www.in.gov/health/files/Reporting_a_Crime_Form_-_February_8_2013.doc) should be used by facility staff/ personnel to report to the IDOH a reasonable suspicion of a crime against a resident or to report retaliation by a facility for reporting of a crime.

For details regarding information to include in the report, as well as regulatory guidance and [Posting of Notice of Employee Rights template](https://www.in.gov/dA/cc8360512a/Notice_under_Affordable_Care_Act_-_August_31_2011.pdf?language_id=1), please see [LTC-Program-Advisory-2011-01-Revised-06-01-22](https://www.in.gov/?a=601600).

The completed report may be submitted to IDOH via:
