# Where can I get my vaccines?

*Health > Immunization > Vaccines For Children (VFC) > Where can I get my vaccines?*

The map can be used to find locations where eligible child and adult Hoosiers can receive publicly funded vaccines through the Vaccine for Children Program. Hover over icons to view vaccine site information and click the legend to filter by provider type. Please reach out to your preferred clinic to find out more information about their scheduling and hours of operations.

For more information about Vaccines for Children (VFC) providers, visit the [Indiana Department of Health Immunization Information Page](https://www.in.gov/health/immunization/vaccines-for-children/index) or the [CDC Information Page](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines-for-children/about/index.html).
