- Public Records Requests

The Indiana Department of Health is subject to the Indiana Access to Public Records Act, IC 5-14-3. If you would like to request records, please submit a public records request:

[Submit a Public Records Request](https://in.accessgov.com/health-apra)

- If you are seeking **a birth or death certificate**, please visit [www.vitalrecords.in.gov](http://www.vitalrecords.in.gov) for more information on placing an order.
- If you are seeking **immunization records**, please visit MyVaxIndiana at [https://eportal.isdh.in.gov/MyVaxIndiana/Default2.aspx](https://eportal.isdh.in.gov/MyVaxIndiana/Default2.aspx).
- **If you are seeking your own medical records, contact the**Indiana Department of Health Public Records Coordinator at publicrecords@health.in.gov.
- **All non-public records media inquiries should be sent to****media@health.in.gov**
