# Mobile Response Operations

*Health > Emergency Preparedness > Mobile Response Operations*

### Mobile Services

DEP has multiple mobile units stationed throughout the State of Indiana and are managed by the Mobile Response Operations section. These units are operated by a team and are trained in a variety of capabilities. Mobile units can be deployed on request to provide or assist in public health service. These services include:

- **Vaccination**
- **Oral Health**
- **Lead testing**
- **School Hearing Screening**

Please click on the links below to submit a survey for the specific service that you are requesting.

## Mobile Services Request

Routine vaccination clinics are available year-round for both adults and children.

Vaccines clinics are available for ages 3 years and up. Anyone under 18 must have a parent or guardian to give consent.

[Request Vaccine Clinic](https://redcap.isdh.in.gov/surveys/?s=WY3DAJ497N9KKH8K)

School hearing screenings are available for grades 1, 4, 7, and 10.

[Request Hearing Screening](https://redcap.isdh.in.gov/surveys/?s=NWE7L7HFY48D73F7)

Basic Lead Testing for children between the ages of 9 months-72 months and pregnant/ suspected pregnant mothers. Daycare Lead testing for children from 1-6 years old is available for licensed daycares only.

[Request Lead Testing](https://redcap.isdh.in.gov/surveys/?s=JH48KX7M4NN8WE3F)

This screening is designed to encourage caregivers to take positive action and ensure timely dental care. These screenings do not take the place of an oral health exam performed by a dental professional.

[Request Oral Health Screenings](https://redcap.isdh.in.gov/surveys/?s=H4EWLLJPYAXWMKCKLJPYAXWMKCK)
