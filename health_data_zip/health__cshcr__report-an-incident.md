# Facility-Reported Incidents

*Health > CSHCR > Facility-Reported Incidents*

| ## About the Complaint and Incident Reporting System | Program Guidance |
| --- | --- |

The Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) is responsible for managing events reported by staff and personnel of comprehensive and residential care facilities. These events ("incidents") are reported through the IDOH [Gateway](https://gateway.isdh.in.gov/Gateway/SignIn.aspx) (*Incident Reporting System*). Within Complaint and Incident Reporting Program reporting guidance, the IDOH Gateway is referred to as the *Incident Reporting System*; however, "Incident Reporting System" is not a distinct application within the IDOH Gateway. The application within the IDOH Gateway is titled *Survey Reporting System*.

You may find links to additional resources and program guidance below.

| ## Report a Complaint | [Report a Complaint](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/report-a-complaint) |
| --- | --- |

Anyone may submit a complaint to IDOH about care provided at any licensed or certified Indiana healthcare facility or entity. IDOH is available to receive complaints against nursing homes, hospitals, home health agencies, hospice agencies, personal service agencies, and intermediate care facilities for persons with intellectual disabilities (ICF-IID; also called group homes). IDOH investigates all eligible complaints.

This report link is to a page that provides information about the complaint process and how to report a complaint. A *complaint* report form is provided for individuals to assist in the reporting of care issues.

| ## Report a Reasonable Suspicion of a Crime | [Report a Reasonable Suspicion of a Crime](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/healthcare-facility-information-center/reporting-a-crime-against-a-resident-information-center) |
| --- | --- |

Federal law requires “covered individuals” to report a reasonable suspicion of a crime against a long-term care resident to IDOH and a local law enforcement entity. Covered individuals include an owner, operator, employee, manager, agent, or contractor of the facility. The included facilities include nursing homes, hospice facilities, or intermediate care facilities for individuals with intellectual disabilities.

This report link is to a page that provides information for long-term care providers about the crime reporting requirements and how to report a reasonable suspicion of a crime against a resident. A *crime* report form is provided for individuals to report suspected crimes to IDOH.

## Reporting Guidance

### Long-Term Care Abuse and Incident Reporting

IDOH's *Long-Term Care Abuse and Incident Reporting* policy ([IDOH-CSHR-LTC-002](https://www.in.gov/health/ltc/files/IDOH-CSHR-LTC-002-12.08.2022-09.01.2027.pdf)) provides guidance for reporting clarity and regulation for comprehensive and residential care facilities, including types of incidents to report, timeline for reporting, and information to include in each report. The initial incident report and follow-up report may be submitted together if all necessary information has been obtained within the timeframe for initial reporting.

Information for an evacuation or event involving the emergency management agency must be reported by phone immediately to 317-464-0038.

### Incident Reporting when the Online System is Down

#### Email

1. Complete the *[Incident Reporting Form](https://www.in.gov/dA/64afef6b93/Incident-Reporting-Form-Fillable.pdf?language_id=1)*
2. Email the completed form to Incidents@health.in.gov
3. Within 24 hours of the *online* *[Incident Reporting System](https://gateway.isdh.in.gov/Gateway/SignIn.aspx)* resuming accessibility, report the incident online and attach the incident report form (from #1) to the report in the Gateway.

#### Voicemail

1. Incident reports are accepted via voicemail only when the Gateway is inaccessible and email is not functioning.

- Please call 1-800-246-8909 and include the following:

- Name of Reporter
- Title of Reporter
- Name of Facility
- Address of Facility
- Type of Incident
- Description of Injury
- Name(s) of Resident(s)
- Name(s) of Staff Involved
- Details of Immediate Action
