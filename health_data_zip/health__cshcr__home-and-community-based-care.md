# Home and Community-Based Care

*Health > CSHCR > Home and Community-Based Care*

| ## About the Division of Home/Community-Based Care     The Division of Home and Community-Based Care is responsible for licensing and certification programs for home health agencies, home health aides (HHAs), personal care services agencies (PSAs), mobile out-of-state healthcare entities, hospice agencies, and end-stage renal disease facilities (ESRD).     The Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) maintains pages for each program. You may find links to the program pages below. | ## Report a Complaint | [Report a Complaint](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/report-a-complaint) |
| --- | --- |

Anyone may submit a complaint to IDOH about care provided at any licensed or certified Indiana healthcare facility or entity. IDOH is available to receive complaints against nursing homes, hospitals, home health agencies, hospice agencies, personal service agencies, and intermediate care facilities for persons with intellectual disabilities (ICF-IID; also called group homes). IDOH investigates all eligible complaints.

This report link is to a page that provides information about the complaint process and how to report a complaint. A *complaint* report form is provided for individuals to assist in the reporting of care issues.

| ## Report an Incident | [Report an Incident](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/report-an-incident) |
| --- | --- |

State or federal regulations require health facilities or entities to report certain types of “incidents” to IDOH. This report link is to a page that provides information about the incident reporting process and how a healthcare facility or entity reports incidents.
