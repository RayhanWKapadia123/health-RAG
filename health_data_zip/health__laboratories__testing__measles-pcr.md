Measles (PCR) Specimen Collection and Transport

No cost for this testing.

1. PCR testing can be completed on the following at the IDOHL: Nasopharyngeal (NP) swabs (recommended), throat swabs.

1. Urine specimens will be forwarded to CDC from the IDOHL.

Reports will be available on LimsNet.
PCR results will be available within 1-2 business days.
