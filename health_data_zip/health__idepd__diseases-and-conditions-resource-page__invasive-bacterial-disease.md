# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: Invasive Bacterial Disease

# Invasive Bacterial Disease

### Overview

Invasive bacterial diseases including [group A streptococcus](https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/diseases-and-conditions-resource-page/streptococcus-group-a-invasive-disease) (*Streptococcus pyogenes*), *[Haemophilus influenzae](https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/diseases-and-conditions-resource-page/haemophilus-influenzae-type-b)*, [*Listeria monocytogenes*](https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/diseases-and-conditions-resource-page/listeriosis), *[Neisseria meningitidis](https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/diseases-and-conditions-resource-page/meningococcal-disease)*, and *[Streptococcus pneumoniae](https://www.in.gov/health/idepd/diseases-and-conditions-resource-page/pneumococcal-infections)* isolated from normally sterile sites are reportable in Indiana residents.

### Normally Sterile Sites

- Blood
- Cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)
- Pleural fluid

- Chest fluid
- Thoracentesis fluid
- Peritoneal fluid

- Abdominal fluid
- Ascites
- Pericardial fluid
- Bone

- Bone marrow
- Joint fluid

- Synovial fluid
- Fluid, needle aspirate, or culture of any specific joint

- Knee
- Ankle
- Elbow
- Hip
- Wrist
- Internal body sites

- Specimen obtained from surgery or aspirate from one of the following

- Lymph node
- Brain
- Heart
- Liver
- Spleen
- Vitreous fluid
- Kidney
- Pancreas
- Ovary
- Vascular tissue

### Exceptions

Group A Streptococcus if the source is

- Muscle
- Tissue or biopsy that is surgically obtained
- **Any**site (even non-sterile sites) in a case of toxic shock syndrome (TSS) or necrotizing fasciitis (NF)
