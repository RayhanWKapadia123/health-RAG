# Methane in Well Water

*Health > EPH > Environmental Hazards > Methane in Well Water*

Methane is a colorless, odorless gas that can dissolve in water. Safety is the main concern with high levels of methane because it is a fire and explosive hazard. Below are some resource materials that provide guidance related to well water on this topic:

- [Informational Brochure](https://www.in.gov/dA/471032dcc0/MethaneInWellWater-4.10.22-AB.pdf?language_id=1)

- [Fact Sheet](https://www.in.gov/dA/03c240fd17/ATSDR_Methane_Fact_Sheet.pdf?language_id=1)
