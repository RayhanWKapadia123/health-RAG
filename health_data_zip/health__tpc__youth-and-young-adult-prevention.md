### 2026 Indiana Youth Tobacco Survey

- The Youth Tobacco Survey (YTS) is a school-based survey administered every other year (fall semester of the even years, such as 2022, 2024, 2026, etc.) that measures cigarette, e-cigarette, cigar, pipe, smokeless tobacco, hookah, nicotine pouch, Delta THC, and cannabis use among Indiana’s youth in grades 6–12.
- The survey also measures the impact of media and advertising, enforcement of youth tobacco access laws, school curriculum, and exposure to secondhand smoke.
- The high school and middle school survey questions may be viewed here:

- [Middle School survey](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/2026IYTS_MS.pdf)
- [High School survey](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/2026IYTS_HS.pdf)
- The survey sample includes **60 high schools and 60 middle schools (grades 6-12)**, and between two to six randomly selected classrooms per school will be surveyed.
- The Indiana YTS is an electronic survey with skip logic, which minimizes the burden on schools and provides an efficient option for survey administration.

- Data are used to plan and evaluate tobacco prevention and cessation programs geared toward youth in Indiana. Results from the 2024 YTS can be found [here](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/surveys-and-reports).
- **Survey participation is voluntary and anonymous**. Student anonymity will be fully protected as no identifying questions are asked, and responses are not linked back to the students’ devices or IP addresses. Data will not be reported by district or school, but rather for Indiana as a whole.
- IDOH works with selected schools to provide clear communication and written requests for parent/guardian consent in advance.

- Schools will receive an unrestricted stipend of **$500** for their participation in the survey.
- Schools have the option of receiving a **school-level report**summarizing their results - helping them understand students’ needs. These reports will only be shared with the school. Schools have the option to survey the entire student body, which will lead to more meaningful data for the school.
