# IOWPA Residential OSS Installer Training Presentations

*Health > EPH > IOWPA Residential OSS Installer Training Presentations*

#### Below are links to the training slides provided by the Indiana Department of Health for IOWPA Installer Certification for Tests A, B and C covering Rule 410 IAC 6-8.3 for residential on-site sewage systems.

[Test A](https://www.in.gov/dA/4b4c288577/IOWPA-Installer-Training-slides-Test-A-2021.pdf?language_id=1) covers general requirements for on-site sewage systems, subsurface trench gravity systems, drainage and dispersal area.

[Test B](https://www.in.gov/dA/b9a3885c19/IOWPA-Installer-Training-slides-Test-B-2021.pdf?language_id=1) covers subsurface trench flood dosed systems, dosing tanks, and effluent pumps.

[Test C](https://www.in.gov/dA/1b6a23cfbb/IOWPA-Installer-Training-slides-Test-C-2021.pdf?language_id=1) covers subsurface trench pressure distribution and elevated sand mound systems.
