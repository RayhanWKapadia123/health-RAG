##### Indiana Specific Reports

- [Impact of smoke-free ordinance strength on smoking prevalence and lung cancer incidence](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0250285)
- [*Effects of Smoking Ban on Acute Myocardial Infarction Hospitalization in Marion County*](http://bmcpublichealth.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12889-018-5153-y#Ack1)
Comparison of heart attack admissions in Marion County and Indianapolis hospitals before and after the Indianapolis smoke-free air law implementation in 2012. Completed by Yi Wang, Ph.D., Anne M. Weaver, PD. 2017
- [*The Economic Impact of Smoke-Free Policies on Business and Health*](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/IUCtrforHealthPolicySmokeFreeReport.pdf)
Indiana University Center for Health Policy report: Smoke-free workplace laws save lives and money without hurting businesses.

### External Evaluation Reports

##### Indiana Air Monitoring Studies

- **[Indianapolis Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/Indianapolis_Air_Monitoring_Study.pdf)**
12/12
Indoor air quality study conducted before and after implementation of smoke-free workplace law in June 2012.
- **[Delaware County Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/DelawareCoFinalReport072812.pdf)**
7/28/12
Indoor air quality study conducted before and after implementation of smoke-free workplace law in August 2011.
- **[Indianapolis Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/Indianapoils_airmonitoring_Oct20091.pdf)**
*10/13/2009*
This study examined indoor air quality in a sample of bars, restaurants, and nightclubs in Indianapolis that permitted smoking.
- **[Fort Wayne Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/FortWayneReport__pre-post_2007.pdf)**
*1/16/2008*
Indoor air quality study conducted before and after implementation of smoke-free workplace law on June 1, 2007.

[Executive Summary of Fort Wayne Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/FW_air_monitoring_summary_Jan2008.pdf)
- **[West Lafayette Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/WestLafayetteReport_pre-post_2008.pdf)**
*3/10/2008*
Indoor air quality study conducted before and after implementation of smoke-free workplace law on July 1, 2007.

[Executive Summary of West Lafayette Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/WL_air_monitoring_summary_Mar2008.pdf)
- **[Grant County Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/GrantCountyairmontoringstudy5.2.08.pdf)**
*5/19/2008*
Indoor air quality study comparing workplaces that allow smoking and workplaces that are smoke-free.

[Executive Summary of Grant County Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/Grant_Co_air_monitoring_summary_May2008.pdf)
- **[New Albany Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/NewAlbanyAirMonitoringReport.pdf)**
5/29/2008
Indoor air quality study comparing workplaces that allowing smoking in New Albany compared to similar workplaces in smoke-free Louisville, KY.

[Executive Summary of New Albany Air Monitoring Study](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/NewAlbany_air_monitoring_summary_May2008.pdf)
- **[Indiana Air Monitoring Study-April 2005](https://www.in.gov/health/tpc/files/research_238.pdf)**
*4/21/2005*
This study examined indoor air quality in a sample of restaurants and bars in 3 Indiana cities, Bloomington, Indianapolis, and Fort Wayne.
