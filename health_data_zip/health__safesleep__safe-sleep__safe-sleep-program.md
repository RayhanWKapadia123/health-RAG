## 100 babies died in Indiana in 2017 of sleep-related deaths.

### Most of these deaths were preventable. What are you doing to help?

Keep checking this site for safe sleep updates. You can receive email alerts when new updates are posted by clicking on the subscribe link below and providing your email information.

[[image: Subscribe for e-mail updates]](https://public.govdelivery.com/accounts/INSDH/subscriber/new?topic_id=INSDH_80)

### Help all Indiana babies reach their first birthdays by following the three steps below:

1. Education

Learn about Safe Sleep, why it matters, and how you can provide infants with a safe sleeping environment.

[Safe Sleep Flipbook](https://www.in.gov/?a=403643)

[AAP Policy on Safe Sleep](https://pediatrics.aappublications.org/content/138/5/e20162938)

More resources can be found [here](https://www.in.gov/health/safesleep/safe-sleep/resources).

2. Implementation

Determine where your organization or agency fits and learn what programs we offer in Indiana to reduce Sudden Unexpected Infant Deaths.

[Safe Sleep Crib Distribution](https://gis.in.gov/apps/isdh/safesleep)

[Fetal-Infant Mortality Review](https://www.in.gov/health/safesleep/fatality-review/fetal-infant-mortality-review)

[Child Fatality Review](https://www.in.gov/health/safesleep/fatality-review/local-child-fatality-review-teams/overview)

3. Reduced rates of infant death in Indiana

See the data for Sudden Unexpected Infant Deaths in Indiana by hospital region [here](https://www.in.gov/health/safesleep/safe-sleep/safe-sleep-data).
