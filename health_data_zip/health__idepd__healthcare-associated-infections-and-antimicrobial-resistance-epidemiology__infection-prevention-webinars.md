# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: HAI-AR Infection Prevention Webinar

# HAI-AR Infection Prevention Webinars

Indiana Department of Health's Healthcare Associated Infections and Antimicrobial Resistance (HAI-AR) team hosts a webinar series to provide relevant information related to HAI and AR organisms and to promote collaboration between federal, state, and local public health professionals and hospital infection prevention teams. Topics include updates regarding healthcare associated infections (HAIs), antimicrobial resistance (AR), *Candida auris*(*C. auris*), and antimicrobial stewardship (AS), and other additional topics. This webinar is designed to inform hospital and long-term care infection preventionists of relevant information and strategies to improve patient safety. These webinars will occur monthly. All slide sets and recordings of previous webinars can be found below.

To register for the HAI/AR Webinar series, please use the following link: [RedCap Registration](https://redcap.isdh.in.gov/surveys/?s=T4EHX8N7DNTN48NJ)
 For more information, email Aubrey West, HAI Epidemiologist I, at AWest@health.in.gov (awest@health.in.gov).
