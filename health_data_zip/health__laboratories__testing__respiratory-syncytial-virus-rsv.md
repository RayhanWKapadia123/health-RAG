Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV) Specimen Collection and Transport

Nasopharyngeal/throat swab is preferred, but nasal/throat washes and aspirates are acceptable for virus isolation.

1. Viral Transport Media (VTM)-available commercially. Some require refrigeration, others do not — check package insert. No dry swabs will be accepted.
2. Swab - Swab material should be synthetic, i.e., rayon, polyester, or Dacron. Calcium alginate or charcoal-impregnated swabs should not be used, nor should wood-shaft swabs.
3. Sterile containers and collection materials for aspirates or washes.
4. Catheter with a suction trap or a bulb aspirator for collecting nasal aspirates.
5. Cold packs or dry ice.
6. Shipping boxes/containers with appropriate shipping labels.

Culture results will be available between 3-15 days.
