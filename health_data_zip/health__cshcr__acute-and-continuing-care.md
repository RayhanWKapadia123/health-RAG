## Notice of Closure or Elimination of Service Lines

### Geriatric Psychiatric Services Closure at Witham Health Services

###### July 20, 2026

Witham Health Services will permanently close its Geriatric Psychiatric Services (GPS) Unit, a 10-bed inpatient program serving adults 65 and older, on Sept. 8, 2026.

Witham does not anticipate any patients remaining in the unit beyond the end of August and expects the region to maintain adequate geriatric psychiatric inpatient capacity.

Current patients will continue to receive care until they are safely discharged, and new admissions will be paused.

### Behavioral Health Service Line Closure at Ascension St. Vincent Warrick

###### July 6, 2026

Ascension St. Vincent Warrick will discontinue its behavioral health service line, including the Inpatient Serenity Unit and Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP), effective Aug. 28, 2026. The Serenity Unit, a 10-bed facility for senior patients needing psychiatric care, and the IOP, serving adults 55 and older, will both cease operations.

All current and incoming patients will continue to receive care until they are safely discharged. The hospital is working closely with regional partners to ensure smooth transitions and ongoing access to behavioral health services. Support and referrals will be provided to patients and families throughout this process.

Ascension St. Vincent Warrick has confirmed to IDOH that it remains committed to the health and well-being of the community and will prioritize dignified, accessible care during this transition.

| ## Report a Complaint | [Report a Complaint](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/report-a-complaint) |
| --- | --- |

Anyone may submit a complaint to IDOH about care provided at any licensed or certified Indiana healthcare facility or entity. IDOH is available to receive complaints against nursing homes, hospitals, home health agencies, hospice agencies, personal service agencies, and intermediate care facilities for persons with intellectual disabilities (ICF-IID; also called group homes). IDOH investigates all eligible complaints.

This report link is to a page that provides information about the complaint process and how to report a complaint. A *complaint* report form is provided for individuals to assist in the reporting of care issues.

| ## Report a Billing Dispute | [Report a Billing Dispute](https://www.in.gov/health/cshcr/acute-and-continuing-care/hospital-site-of-service-billing-disputes) |
| --- | --- |

Reports of billing disputes are intended to be submitted for disputes between insurers and hospitals for site-of-service or institutional/standardized billing by location.

If you are an insurer, health maintenance organization (HMO), employer, or anyone responsible for paying for healthcare services, you may submit a complaint if there is an issue with the submission of a bill for health services from a qualified provider.
