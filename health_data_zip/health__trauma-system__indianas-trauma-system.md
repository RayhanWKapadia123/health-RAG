**Trauma System Development Grant Opportunities**

The Indiana Department of Health’s (IDOH’s) Division of Trauma and Injury Prevention are providing funding to support quality improvement projects and the ongoing development of the statewide trauma system. For more information regarding these opportunities such as how to apply, what are the requirements or any other questions, please click on the link [here](https://indianactsi.org/funding/opportunities/funding-grants/#TCC202706).
