The Division of HIV, STI & Viral Hepatitis works to promote public health and enhance the quality of life for Indiana residents through the prevention, intervention and treatment of HIV and other sexually transmitted infections. We envision the elimination of the transmission of HIV and other STIs and the provision of the equitable distribution of health resources to ensure a quality of life for each infected individual.
