# 2024 Ambulatory Surgical Center Utilization Reports

*Health > CSHCR > Acute and Continuing Care > ambulatory-outpatient-surgical-centers-asc-licensing-and-certification-program > 2024 Ambulatory Surgical Center Utilization Reports*

On an annual basis, the Indiana Department of Health (IDOH) requires ambulatory surgical centers to provide utilization statistics and outcomes regarding their facility. This information is collected via an electronic health data portal system, converted to Adobe PDF format, displayed on this site, and is updated annually.
This page is updated as reports are received.
