# Order Certificates

*Health > Vital Records > Order Certificates*

## Attention Customers:

**For faster service,** **place your order through the local health department**where the event occurred. View local health department information [here.](https://www.in.gov/health/health-and-human-services/local-health-department-outreach-division/local-health-department-information/)

**Walk-in service is NOT available at the Indiana Department of Health.**

To purchase a birth or death certificate in person, please visit the Local Health Department where the birth or death occurred. [View](https://www.in.gov/health/lhd/local-health-department-map) local health department information.

##### Order Online

 [Order records online](https://www.in.gov/health/vital-records/order-now/vital-records-online-vitalchek-network)

These orders will be processed through VitalChek Network, Inc. VitalChek is an independent company that the Indiana Department of Health Division of Vital Records has partnered with to provide this service, and is the only IDOH-authorized and approved vendor for online ordering.

##### Order by Mail:

###### Birth Certificates

Please print [State Form 49607, Application for Search and Certified Copy of Birth Record](https://www.in.gov/dA/d8ff3a6fef/Birth-Request-10.2018.doc?language_id=1).

The form includes applicable [fees](https://www.in.gov/health/vital-records/), identification requirements, and the mailing address. The cost for the first certificate is $10.00 and $4.00 for each additional copy. Please make checks payable to the Indiana Department of Health.

###### Death Certificates

Please print [State Form 49606, Application for Search of Certified or Non-Certified Copy of a Death Record](https://www.in.gov/dA/fa6449db09/Death-Request-10.2018.doc?language_id=1).

The form includes applicable [fees](https://www.in.gov/health/vital-records/), identification requirements, and mailing the address. The cost for the first certificate is $8.00 and $4.00 for each additional copy. Please make checks payable to the Indiana Department of Health.

##### Order by Phone

To order a birth or death certificate by phone and pay with a credit card, please call (866) 601-0891.

This is a toll-free number that is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to place certificate orders and provide customer service support through a reputable third-party company.
