# Infectious Disease Epidemiology & Prevention Division: Zoonotic Diseases

# Zoonotic Diseases

Zoonotic diseases (also known as zoonoses) are caused by germs that spread between animals and people. Zoonotic diseases are caused by harmful germs like viruses, bacterial, parasites, and fungi. These germs can cause many different types of illnesses in people and animals. Animals can sometimes appear healthy even when they are carrying germs that can make people sick, depending on the zoonotic disease.
