IGRA Testing for Tuberculosis

QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus Specimen Collection, Packaging and Shipping

$20, or provide valid insurance information.

1. Required specimen: Whole blood collected in QFT-Plus tubes

2. Minimum volume required: 1 mL for each tube (Nil, TB1, TB2, Mitogen)

3. Specimens lacking complete information will not be tested until the missing information has been provided.

4. QFT-Plus tubes must be incubated at 37°C (± 1°C) within 16 hours of collection. Incubation may be completed at the collection site or at the testing laboratory. Tubes must not be frozen.

1. Label each QFT-Plus tube with the patient name, DOB, and date and time of collection, including a minimum of two unique identifiers on the tube.

2. Collect 1 mL of blood by venipuncture directly into each QFT-Plus collection tube:

Tubes should be at room temperature (17-25° C) at the time of collection.

1 mL tubes draw slowly, so be sure to keep the tube on the needle for 2-3 seconds once the tube appears to have completed filling.

The black box on the side of the tube indicates the correct filling volume (0.8 mL to 1.2 mL). Ensure that the tubes are filled within this range. If the blood level is not in this range, a new tube should be collected.

If a butterfly needle is being used to collect blood, a purge tube should be used first to ensure that the tubing is filled with blood prior to the QFT-Plus collection tubes being filled.

3. Immediately after filling the tubes, shake them 10 times just firmly enough to make sure the inner surface of the tube is coated in blood in order to dissolve the antigens on the tube walls.

**Caution: Overly vigorous shaking may cause gel disruption and could lead to aberrant results.**

 4. After collection, shaken QFT-Plus tubes must be transferred to a 37°C (± 1°C) incubator as soon as possible and within 16 hours of collection.

Option 1: Ship tubes to the testing laboratory so as to arrive within 16 hours of collection. The tubes should be stored and transported at 17-25°C.

Option 2: Incubate tubes at the site of collection. If the tubes are not incubated immediately after collection, remix the tubes by inverting them 10 times immediately prior to incubation. Incubate the tubes upright for 16-24 hours at 37°C (± 1°C). After incubation, tubes may be held between 4°C and 27°C for up to 3 days prior to centrifugation at the testing laboratory.

5. QFT-Plus tubes may be centrifuged at the collection site or the testing laboratory. Tubes should be centrifuged for 15 minutes at 2000-3000 RFT (g). Centrifuged QFT-Plus tubes are stable for up to 28 days when stored and shipped at 2-8°C.

 6. Ensure the collection tubes are capped securely. Leaking specimens are unacceptable for testing and will be discarded.

 7. Complete the LimsNet test request for Tuberculosis (QuantiFERON-TB), ensuring that the patient information on the specimen tube matches the information entered into the submission form. Note whether the tubes have been incubated or centrifuged prior to shipment.

All patient specimens should be labeled, packaged and shipped in accordance with federal shipping regulations, using Biological Substances, Category B.

1. Place the blood collection tubes in the specimen transport bag with absorbent material. Seal the bag using the closure at the top. Up to six tubes may be sent in one shipper.

2. Place the LimsNet coversheet in the outer pouch of the specimen transport bag or outside of the insulated shipper to prevent contamination from the specimen.

3. Place the specimen transport bag and frozen ice packs inside of the insulated shipper and close the outer shipping box. The specimens should be placed between two ice packs with padding (such as paper towels) between the tubes and the ice packs.

4. The outer shipping box should be labelled using an IDOHL 6A label and then placed inside of the UPS Laboratory Pak bag.

5. Affix the pre-addressed shipping label to the outer packaging. Specimens may be shipped via commercial service, such as UPS or FedEx or sent by STAT courier. Specimens should be shipped to arrive at IDOHL Monday through Friday. Specimens should not be shipped to be in transit during the weekend or holiday.

6. Alternatively, specimens may be delivered to IDOHL during normal business hours (8:15 am to 4:45 pm). Specimens delivered by courier or LHD personnel must meet the temperature criteria specified above.
