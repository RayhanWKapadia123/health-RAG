# Quit Now Indiana

*Health > TPC > Quit Now Indiana*

Quit Now Indiana is the frontline tool for helping Hoosiers end nicotine dependence. Quit Now Indiana is working to reduce the use of all commercial tobacco products in Indiana. The network of service programs, which is available for free to all Hoosiers 13 and older, is here to improve to health of Hoosiers and end exposure to tobacco and addiction.

Quit Now Indiana also works with [healthcare providers](https://www.quitnowindiana.com/providers) and [employers](https://www.quitnowindiana.com/healthy-businesses) to help connect patients and employees with these free services, and to integrate with established electronic health records or existing employee tobacco cessation programs.

**For more information, please visit [QuitNowIndiana.com](https://www.quitnowindiana.com/)**
