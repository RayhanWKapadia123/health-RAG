## Comprehensive Outpatient Rehabilitation Facility Directory

A comprehensive outpatient rehabilitation facility is established and operated at a single fixed location exclusively for the purpose of providing diagnostic, therapeutic, and restorative services to outpatients by and under the supervision of a physician.
