##### Basic NIMS/ICS Training

- [IDOH NIMS and ICS Training Delivery Plan](https://www.in.gov/dA/3e87cb729f/Indiana-Department-of-Health-ICS-TTT-Plan.pdf?language_id=1)
- Applications to enroll in the IDOH ICS Training Program – [Apply Here](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/4ffe3e8e72324609a40ab75d0f21b8a8)

NIMS and ICS courses are nationally recognized basic National Incident Management Training courses provided by FEMA's Emergency Management Institute. Completion of these courses is free for the end user and will allow the participant to gain a basic understanding of Incident Command and the National Incident Management System.

- [IS-100.C: Introduction to the Incident Command System, ICS 100](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-100.c&lang=en)
- [IS-200.C: Basic Incident Command System for Initial Response, ICS-200](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-200.c&lang=en)
- [IS-700.B: An Introduction to the National Incident Management System](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-700.b&lang=en)
- [IS-800.D: National Response Framework, An Introduction](https://training.fema.gov/is/courseoverview.aspx?code=IS-800.d&lang=en)

### Course Registration Identification

##### Public Safety Identification (PSID)

A Public Safety Identification (PSID) is required to register for the [Indiana Public Safety Portal – Acadis](https://acadisportal.in.gov/). Acadis is the consolidated location for a variety of trainings around the State of Indiana by multiple disciplines.

- [Obtain a PSID.](https://www.in.gov/dhs/3880.htm) Your PSID and Acadis login information will be emailed to you. If you have forgotten your PSID, click [here](https://www.in.gov/dhs/3207.htm) and select “PSID Information Lookup."

##### Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) Student Identification (SID)

A FEMA Student Identification is required for registration of federal courses offered through the [Federal Emergency Management Agency](https://training.fema.gov/).

- [Obtain a FEMA SID](https://cdp.dhs.gov/femasid/register). If you have forgotten your FEMA SID, click [here](https://cdp.dhs.gov/femasid) and select “Forgot Student ID?”
