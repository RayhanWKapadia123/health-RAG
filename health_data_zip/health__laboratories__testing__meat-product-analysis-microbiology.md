Meat Product Analysis (Microbiology)

Analysis of meat products for pathogenic *E. coli, Listeria, Salmonella* and *Campylobacter*.

Meat, poultry and environmental swab samples submitted for microbiological analyses will only be accepted from the Indiana State Board of Animal Health Meat and Poultry Division and other government agencies.

 Contact the Indiana Board of Animal Health Meat and Poultry Division for sample containers and instructions.
