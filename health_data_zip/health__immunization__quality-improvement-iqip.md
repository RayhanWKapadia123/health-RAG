# Quality Improvement (IQIP)

*Health > Immunization > Quality Improvement (IQIP)*

[image: iqip]IQIP is a 12-month process where public health representatives and VFC providers collaborate to identify QI strategies to increase vaccine uptake by improving and enhancing immunization workflow.

##### The Initial Site Visit

- Observe provider workflow
- Review initial coverage
- Select QI strategies
- Provide technical assistance
- Choose action items for the strategy implementation plan

##### 2 Month and 6 Month check-in

- Review progress toward strategy implementation
- Provide technical assistance
- Update strategy implementation plan

##### 12 Month Follow Up

- Review progress toward strategy implementation
- Provide technical assistance
- Review year-over-year coverage change

##### IQIP Tips

- Schedule the next vaccine with the patient prior to them leaving the office
- Provide the patient with their vaccine information
- Provide patient reminders when their next vaccine is due
- Provide positive education to patients and providers on vaccines
- Break the vaccine myths and assist patients in understanding the truth about the vaccines
